WELCOME TO OCHI SAFARI LODGE

Retreat from the city. Connect with nature.

Ochi Safari Lodge is one of the finest and oldest resort areas in Zimbabwe, designed to offer peace and tranquility in an unmatched setting of greenery, vast open spaces and breathtaking scenery.

Located just 32km from Harare CBD, Ochi is the ideal destination for conferences, accommodation, events, corporate retreats, family outings and unforgettable leisure experiences.
